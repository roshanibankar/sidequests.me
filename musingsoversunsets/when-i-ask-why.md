---
title: When I ask "why"
date: 2023
type: poem
---

When I ask "why",  
Tempers may rise & some may sign.  
Then why find the answers that will fade when the questions shift?  
Like old truths lost in the drift.  

So I wonder, why I wonder, why I wonder keen,  
Hoping for answers to understand what may be forever unseen.  

And if I ask a wrong question someday,  
Will you stay with me until we frame it another way?  


**By Rosh**
