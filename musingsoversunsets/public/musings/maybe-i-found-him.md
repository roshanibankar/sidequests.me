---
title: Maybe I Found Him
date: 2023-02-28
type: poem
---

Words like embers, comfort and inspire,  
But actions fans the flames, set hearts on fire.  
A bond we forge an intellectual spark,  
Yet timing's hand could leave its cruelest mark.

I yearn to share, explore the world unseen,  
His thoughts, his tales, a tapestry serene.  
But time, a thief, steals moments meant to be,  
Leaving whispers of "what ifs" in my memory.

A million ears may hear his stories told,  
But can I truly understand, unfold the depths within, the meaning they impart,  
Share a resonance that echoes his heart.

His story sings a melody profound,  
A symphony of thoughts on sacred ground.  
Unfamiliar notes that seal my soul,  
A connection forged, yet left to take its toll.

So I hold on tight to moments shared in time,  
Let understanding bloom, a love sublime.  
For even if the path diverges far away,  
The memory of connection lights the day.


**By Rosh**
