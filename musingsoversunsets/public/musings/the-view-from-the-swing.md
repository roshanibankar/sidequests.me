---
title: The View From The Swing
date: 2026-04-25
type: poem
---
![The View From The Swing](/sunsets/sunset9.jpg)

I saw two people on a swing tonight,  
Moving gently in the dark, under the light.  
Once I would have smiled and thought it's sweet,  
Wishing I had someone's hand to meet.

But now my mind goes to darker places,  
As I look close and try to read their faces.  
Are they whispering words of love and peace?  
Or is the quiet night the only time they found  
To fix a love that’s breaking on the ground?  

Did they look up and see a star?  
Or have their minds long drifted afar?  
Like the people who just walked away,  
Who let the heavy world take all their time,  
And turned their happy chime to gray.  

I wonder how much pain I’ve had to endure,  
To look at love and not see any cure,  
Until I learned how love is always there,
How it just lingers rent-free in the air.

So now when the swing is empty, 
I peek to look at it close,  
And notice how it lowkey looks like me.

**By Rosh**
