---
title: My Spark
date: 2026-04-05
type: poem
---

I want to write, but I feel no spark,  
Like my magic was stolen and locked in the dark.  
I dive so deep just to end up ashore,  
No matter how far,  
I’m pushed back once more.  

Are the waves guiding me?  
Or are mirages dressed up as hopeful dreams?  
I search for my feelings in the grains of sand,  
Finding empty shells in my hand was not what I planned.  

I dive back again,  
Not hunting the ache,  
Or counting what's spent.  
Only to find a pearl,  
And when found,  
It shines in my hand like it always knew its worth.  

I want to write of its light, keep it close, keep it true,  
Hide it from the world and give it to you.  
Now I see why the shore pulled me free  
Cause some things aren’t lost.  
They’re just found differently.

**By Rosh**
