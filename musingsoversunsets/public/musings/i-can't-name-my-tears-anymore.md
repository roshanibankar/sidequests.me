---
title: I Can't Name My Tears Anymore
date: 2026-04-05
type: poem
---

I sit with the weight I cannot name,  
A quiet river behind my eyes,  
Not angry, not sad, not fully tamed  
Just liquid threads of buried skies.  

They don’t need reasons.  
They don’t need labels.  
I let the tears fall,  
Soft as whispers.  

Each droplet remembers a story I forgot,  
A wound, a loss, a laugh, a song,  
Yet I won’t ask for it to explain.  
I only let it pass,  
And I pass too,  
Into a space where grief can breathe,  
Where numbness softens,  
And I recognize not the tears,  
But the quiet courage of letting them be.

**By Rosh**
