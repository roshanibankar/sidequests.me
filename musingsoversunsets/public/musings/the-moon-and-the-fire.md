---
title: The Moon & The Fire
date: 2023-03-22
type: poem
---


In the dance of night, where shadows loom,  
I'm fire, and you are the moon.  

I flicker and blaze with untamed grace,  
While you shimmer softly in the dark's embrace.  

Can we ever find our way,  
Amidst the stars, where we both sway?  
Different elements on different paths, yet hope still gleams,  
Afraid to try, yet longing it seems.  

Why must the world keep us apart,  
When all we crave is to share our heart?  
Do you ever think of me, out there alone?  
Or am I just a star in your vast unknown?  

Though we were never bound by a thread,  
I'll wait for the moment where dreams are fed.  

Cause who's to say the moon won't catch fire someday,  
Like an unexpected encounter here to stay.
