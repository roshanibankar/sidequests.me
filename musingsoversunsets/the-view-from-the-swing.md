---
title: The View from the Swing
date: 2026-04-25
type: poem
---

I saw two people on a swing tonight,  
Moving gently in the dark, under the light.  
Once I would have smiled and thought it's sweet,  
And dreamed for my hands and his to meet.  

But now my mind goes to a darker place.  
I look at them and try to read their face.  
Are they whispering words of love and peace,  
Or trying to fight a fight that will not cease?  
Is the quiet night the only time they found  
To fix a love that’s breaking on the ground?  

I wonder how much pain I’ve had to bear,  
To look at love and only see a tear,  
Until he showed me how love doesn't ever end.

Did they look up and see a star?  
Or are their minds long lost way too far?  
I think of people who just walked away,  
Who left the swing because they couldn't play,  
Who let the heavy world take all their time,  
And turned their happy rhythm into a crime.  

Like those who shout their thoughts into thin air, #damn there just wont be another shakespeare wow 
Who force their words and do not seem to care,  
Who never let another person speak,  
And leave the quiet ones so tired and weak.  

So now when the swing is empty, 
I sit in the dark and observe it,  
'Cause it lowkey looks like me.
