---
title: Delay (A Digital Drift)
date: 2024-08-29
type: poem
---

Seconds stretch like years,  
When the messages sent linger in digital queues,  
I send my heart in typed out words,  
Hoping it reaches you undistorted & unused.  

Then I'll wonder if I’m losing you,  
To the silence that fills the space,  
Or if we’re just the victims of this age,  
Where time is fast but still misplaced.  

Is it the Wi-Fi, or is it me?  
Did I say something wrong, make you pause?  
I replay our words, our laughs, our fights,  
Trying to pinpoint where we lost the cause.  

I wait for replies that take their time,  
Thinking that emotions begin to fade,  
In the gaps between the words we’re lucky to even share,  
But I realize that these connections though delayed are still rare.  

Even tho this digital delay may sting,  
Our delay is brief, like a blink, a pause,  
But compared to days in forgotten ink,  
It seems like a luxury in disguise,  
Cause even in the missed moments,  
We’re closer than their long goodbyes.  


**By Rosh**
